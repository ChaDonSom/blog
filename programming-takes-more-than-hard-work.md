# Programming takes more than hard work

Man, I'm finding out this:

Programming requires more foresight than hard work. Not that it doesn't take hard work, it sure is hard and a lot of hard work. But you make more hard work for yourself by not knowing.

But it's not just plain foresight. You have to have foresight to see what can work; but really you have to have this "compartmentalization" ability to forget about the pros & cons you were just weighing for the "foresight", and come down on a process/tool and do the hard work. You have to trust your own 1000-ft leadership and you have to have those 2 separate modes of working, leader mode and worker mode.

This came out of trying to figure out `MomentCast`​ for Pinia ORM. When I started using Pinia ORM, it was because the querying saves a lot of mental trouble of manual JS sorting and filtering on the reactive data. But now, it has cost me almost 3 days of work because I can't figure out their mental model for how data should move into and out of the store.

This has been 3 days of hard work, but not much to show for it. So, even though it's been hard work, it hasn't done much. "Perfect practice makes perfect".

Your results are due more to foresight than hard work.
