# Chapter whiteboards

## 1 Corinthians 9 topics

Paul's authority; its attestation by the Word/Moses; order of labor; spiritual ministers ordained to live by physical results of it; Paul's compulsion to preach; his desire to share it for free; how he becomes like all people, to gain more; his reason (to take part alongside them); how hard he runs (and we should run) - like a race; importance of bodily control (avoiding being cast away).

## Leviticus 15 topics

A man with a running issue (e.g. bleeding): what to consider unclean around him (furniture, dishes, etc.). His reconciliation. Unclean items from copulation; from a woman having issuing blood; her reconciliation. How others can maintain cleanliness around them.

## Mark 1 topics

Prophecy of John's coming, Jesus' baptism, 40 days fasting, and preaching, His healing of many, of Simon's mother, of the first leper; His calling disciples from fishing boats; His casting the unclean spirit out of a man in the synagogue.

## Proverbs 20 topics

Addiction, anger, strife, inner wisdom, kingship, righteousness vs. iniquity, the difficulty of percieving the thoughts & intents of the heart, trusting in the Lord, the value of punishment.
